Tinos is a metric-compatible substitute for Times New Roman. Oswald is a condensed display alternative to Impact, including Cyrillic; it is not metrically identical. Anton includes Latin and Cyrillic. All bundled font files include the complete Russian alphabet, including Ё/ё. SHA-256 checksums are recorded in manifest.json.
