**Goal:** Port shared office editing with local bytes, current ACL, locks, durable recovery, and preserved external-change/exit warnings. Production remains legacy until complete acceptance.

**Architecture:** Per-document durable office identity captures original revision and digest. Per-user opaque hashed capabilities bind to the issuing authenticated browser session; logout/password change/ACL revocation invalidate subsequent operations. Shared locks expire after30min; session drift retires the WOPI identity without discarding pending recovery. Local saves use SaveJournal with an internal office session binding; other editor paths are fenced while an active/pending office identity exists. Discovery/HTTP/PutFile/UI follow after core invariants pass.

Timestamp/retry continuation: OfficeSaves requires the client's last-modified precondition for changed nonempty originals, compares only against the pinned current revision, and retains a prepared recovery copy on mismatch. Receiving identical bytes acknowledges the existing current revision in the guarded ingress transaction without another document version, preview job or save event. Its durable acknowledgement is the committed ingress row plus the unchanged immutable current version; it does not create a second SaveJournal row for a write that did not occur. A retry via a new database connection follows the same rule. Historical content hashes are deliberately not treated as retries: intentional Undo with the current precondition creates a new version. UTC fractional zero padding and +00:00 are accepted without truncating nonzero submillisecond differences.
